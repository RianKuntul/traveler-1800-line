Entry for the make.fun **One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800** bounty: write one sentence, at most 200 characters, that explains the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — no words coined after their time, no list of features, just a picture they could actually hold in their head.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`).

## The entry sentence

> **Picture the whole world as one great library whose every book sits open on your table, and whose every letter reaches any door before the ink dries.**

One image a person from 1800 already knows — a library, a letter, a table — carrying the two things the internet really is: every book ever written open in front of anyone, and any message reaching any door faster than ink dries.
